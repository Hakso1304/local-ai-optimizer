# SATURATION GATE: KEEP OR REMOVE?
It is **not justified as a necessary condition for spill or protection**. The live guard uses adjustedSpill with pinned/baseline subtraction, but has **no independent raw-growth check** (B:641–642). Raw growth is only a later inter-rung cliff rule (D:98–105), unavailable for a first rung and bypassed for failed requests (D:42–52). Successful persistent retries rescue the reported metric, not protection during an attempt.
Keep saturation only as disclosed supporting evidence, not a switch that converts observed residual residency to zero or lets advisory ceilings determine aborts. Replacement needs aligned per-sample residuals, a verified benign baseline, growth/duration/pressure evidence and explicit unknown handling. Simply labelling all raw shared bytes spill would also be wrong: pinned allocations and transient placement exist. N1 proves estimate-dependent classification, not that the physical residency was harmless; N2 proves a safety/evidence blind spot, not hardware-confirmed spill.
